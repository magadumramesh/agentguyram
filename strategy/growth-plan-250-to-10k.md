# @agentguyram: 90-Day Growth Plan (250 → 10K)

**Window:** Sep 28 – Dec 27, 2026 (13 weeks)
**Goal:** 10,000 followers (~40x)
**Written:** Sep 25, 2026

---

## 1. Where you are today (audit of `agentguyram-media`)

Here's what the 13 post folders from Sep 6–23 show:

| What's working | What's holding growth back |
|---|---|
| Strong, recognisable design (dark "Anatomy of an Agent" and light "Ace Agentic Coding" / "Signal Check" styles) | **Almost everything is a carousel.** Carousels deepen trust with people who already follow you. They are poor at reaching people who don't. Reels reach roughly 3–5x more accounts per post. |
| Named series ("Anatomy of an Agent Part 2", "Ace Agentic Coding Day 9") | **Your face is a 60px avatar.** People follow a *person*. There's no talking-head or voice content yet. |
| Clear "SAVE THIS" / "SWIPE" CTAs | **Titles are insider-y**: "Add Login Is Not A Plan", "Sizing The Task Right", "Two Ways to Fork a Subagent". They're great for existing followers, but a stranger scrolling past doesn't know why they should care. |
| Real, useful agentic-coding substance | **Inconsistent cadence**: gaps on Sep 7, 8, 11, 12, 14 and 19, then two posts on Sep 15. |
| One timely news post (OpenAI "Critical" threshold) | **The CTAs ask for saves, but shares matter more.** DM sends are the #1 signal for reaching non-followers. |

**The main diagnosis:** your content quality is ahead of your distribution. You don't need better carousels. You need a **reach engine (Reels)** feeding the carousels you already make well.

## 2. The math (be honest with yourself)

You need about 9,750 new followers in 90 days, which is about **110 per day** on average.

- A good Reel converts about **0.3–1%** of non-follower viewers into followers.
- So you need about **1.5–3M non-follower views** across the quarter.
- In practice that means **~2–4 breakout Reels (100K–500K views)** plus a steady base of 5–20K-view Reels.

You can't get there with 1 post/day of carousels. You can get there with **4 Reels and 3 carousels a week (one post a day), plus aggressive iteration on whatever hits.**

**Milestones (so you know by week 4 if you're on track):**

| Date | Stretch (10K path) | Solid (still a win) |
|---|---|---|
| Oct 25 (wk 4) | 1,200 | 700 |
| Nov 22 (wk 8) | 4,000 | 2,000 |
| Dec 27 (wk 13) | **10,000** | 4,500 |

Growth is lumpy. One Reel will do 80% of the work. The plan is built to give yourself **as many shots at that Reel as possible**.

## 4. Content system: 3 pillars, 2 formats

| Pillar | Purpose | Format | Share of posts |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Watch it work** (demos) | Reach + "whoa" factor, most shareable | **Reel**: screen recording + your face/voice | 40% |
| **2. Signal Check** (news, fast) | Timely reach, "send this to…" | **Reel** first, carousel follow-up | 25% |
| **3. Ace Agentic Coding / Anatomy** (teach) | Saves, trust, follows from profile visits | **Carousel** (your current strength) | 35% |

### Pillar 1: "Watch it work" Reels (your biggest opportunity)
Formula: **outcome first → the prompt/setup → the result → the lesson.**
- 20–45 seconds, your face in the first 2 seconds (split-screen or picture-in-picture over the screen recording).
- On-screen text hook plus voiceover. Sped-up agent footage at 4–8x.
- End on a *transferable* lesson, not "follow for more".

### Pillar 2: Signal Check (speed wins)
- When a big agent/model release drops (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, major open-source), **post a Reel within 6 hours**. The first 3 creators get most of the views.
- Angle: "What it *actually* means for people who build", not a press-release rewrite.
- Keep a ready-to-go Signal Check Reel template (intro, 3 points, "my take") so you only swap the content.

### Pillar 3: Teaching carousels (keep, but repackage)
- Keep your series. Rewrite slide 1 around the **viewer's pain**, not the concept:
  - ❌ "Sizing The Task Right" → ✅ "Your agent isn't dumb. You're giving it the wrong-sized task."
  - ❌ "Add Login Is Not A Plan" → ✅ "Why your agent writes garbage when you ask for 'add login'"
  - ❌ "Two Ways to Fork a Subagent" → ✅ "90% of Claude Code users never touch this feature"
- Put your real face (bigger) on slide 1 or the last slide.
- The last slide should ask for a **share**: "Send this to the dev who still types every line by hand."

| Day | Post | Notes |
|---|---|---|

| Tue | Carousel: Ace Agentic Coding | Series episode |
| Wed | Reel: Signal Check or quick tip | Swap in news if something big dropped |
| Thu | Carousel: Anatomy of an Agent | Deeper explainer |
| Fri | Reel: Watch it work | |
| Sat | Reel: remix of the week's best performer | New hook, same idea |
| Sun | Carousel: Sunday Cheat Sheet | The save magnet. News roundups go in Stories instead |

Plus **daily Stories**: 3–5 frames (behind the scenes, polls, "which should I build next?"). Stories keep existing followers warm and generate the two-way conversation signal.

**Batching:** record 4 Reels in one 2-hour session (Sat/Sun). Make carousels with your existing template pipeline.

## 6. Packaging rules (apply to every post)

1. **Hook in 1.5 seconds.** Text on screen *and* spoken. Use one of these patterns:
   - Result-first: "This agent fixed 14 bugs while I was in a meeting."
   - Contrarian: "Stop writing prompts. Write this file instead."
   - Mistake: "You're using Claude Code wrong if you do this."
   - Stakes: "An AI just did something no AI has done before." (your OpenAI post already does this well)
2. **Design for sends, not likes.** Ask: "Who would someone DM this to?" Say it: "Send this to your team lead."
3. **Watch-time:** cut every pause. Put the payoff at the end so people stay. Loop the last frame into the first.
4. **Captions = search.** Hashtags barely matter now; Instagram search reads captions and on-screen text. Use keywords people type: *Claude Code, AI agents, agentic coding, Cursor, Codex, MCP, AI automation*. Add 3–5 niche hashtags max.
5. **Original only.** Never repost others' clips. No TikTok/CapCut watermarks. Always include audio.
6. **One post per day, max.** Two posts on the same day (like Sep 15) cannibalise each other.

## 7. Distribution: what you do *besides* posting

**a) Comment-keyword lead magnet (turns views into follows)**
- Build a free **"Agent Starter Kit"**: your best CLAUDE.md template, 5 skills, a subagent recipe and a verification checklist. **Host it in this repo**, so the repo *becomes* the lead magnet.
- In Reels/carousels: "Comment **AGENT** and I'll DM you the kit." Set up a DM auto-reply tool (e.g. ManyChat) to send it, and require a follow for the link.
- Comments and DMs both boost ranking, and the kit gives people a reason to follow.

**b) Collab posts (the fastest borrowed audience)**
- List 15 creators in adjacent niches (AI tools, dev productivity, indie hackers, tech careers) with 5K–100K followers.
- Pitch each one a *specific* co-created post: "I'll build X with an agent on your idea; we post as a Collab." Aim for 1 collab a week from week 3.

**c) 30 minutes a day of smart commenting**
- Leave genuinely useful comments (not "great post 🔥") on the latest posts of 10 big AI/dev accounts *within their first hour*. This is the cheapest profile traffic there is.

**d) Reply to every comment and DM within the first 60 minutes after posting.** Two-way conversation is now a confirmed ranking signal.

**e) Recycle everywhere (zero extra effort)**
- Every Reel also goes to YouTube Shorts, TikTok and LinkedIn (download a clean, watermark-free export). LinkedIn especially loves agentic-coding content and drives Instagram follows.

**f) Trial Reels**
- Use Trial Reels (shown only to non-followers) to A/B test hooks: same video, 2–3 different openings. Check in-app whether your account is eligible yet (older rules required 1K followers; newer reports say all accounts). If it isn't, it unlocks at the week-4 milestone.

## 9. Metrics to track weekly (15 min every Sunday)

Track these in `strategy/weekly-tracker.md`:

| Metric | Why | Healthy benchmark |
|---|---|---|
| **Sends per reach** | #1 signal for non-follower reach | >1% is great |
| **Avg watch % (Reels)** | #1 signal overall | >50% for 30s Reels |
| **Follows per 1K non-follower reach** | Is content converting? | 5–10+ |
| Saves per reach (carousels) | Trust/depth | >2% |
| Non-follower reach % | Is the reach engine working? | >60% on Reels |
| Net followers/week | The goal | See milestones (§2) |

**Double-down rule:** any post that does **3x your median** reach gets 3 remixes (new hook, new angle, new format) within 10 days.
**Kill rule:** any format that is below median for 4 posts in a row gets dropped.

## 11. Things to stop

- Posting 2 pieces on the same day
- Titles only existing followers would understand
- "SAVE THIS" as the only CTA (rotate in "Send this to…" and "Comment AGENT")
- Going 2+ days without posting in the first 60 days
- Buying followers, engagement pods or follow/unfollow. They wreck your reach-to-engagement ratios, which the algorithm uses.
