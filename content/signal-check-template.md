# Signal Check template (news Reels)

Use this when a major agent/model release drops. **Aim to post within 6 hours**: the first 3 creators get most of the views.

## Rules
- A **primary source only** (the company's blog, docs or changelog). No reposting other creators' clips.
- Your angle: **"What it means for people who build,"** not a press-release rewrite. Use the PM lens: who is this for, what does it replace, what would you actually try?
- No hype words ("game-changer", "insane", "RIP developers").
- Every number on screen is quoted from the source and named in the first comment.
