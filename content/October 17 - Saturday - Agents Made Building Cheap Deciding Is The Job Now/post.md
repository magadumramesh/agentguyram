## Caption
AI agents made building software cheap. That's exactly the problem.

When building was expensive, you had to choose. Now the agent will build anything you ask, so you build everything, and the product gets worse.

At work, half my job is saying no to good ideas. On weekends I forgot to.

My rule now: before I open the agent, one line. "Who asked for this, and what will they stop doing?" No answer means no task.
