- Illustration: 5 role cards in a fan: ENGINEER · PM · DESIGNER · MARKETER · FOUNDER
- Bottom: "Find your role." / **"Try one task this week."**

**S2 · Engineer**
1. Test-first bug fixes (write the failing test, then the fix)
2. Codebase tours for unfamiliar modules
3. Overnight lint / test-coverage PRs

**S3 · Product manager**
1. Clickable prototypes before PRDs
2. Clustering customer feedback, with quotes
3. Weekly stakeholder updates from commits + tickets

**S4 · Designer**
1. Mock → real component with every state
2. Accessibility audit of a page
3. Copy variants in the product's voice

**S5 · Marketer**
1. Landing-page variants you can actually deploy
2. Competitor page teardown into a comparison table
3. Analytics exports → "what changed this week" summary

**S6 · Founder / solo builder**
1. Release notes that write themselves
2. Support-reply drafts (you approve and send)
3. Internal admin tools in an afternoon

**S7 · The rule for every role**
- "The agent drafts. You decide. Anything customer-facing gets your eyes first."
