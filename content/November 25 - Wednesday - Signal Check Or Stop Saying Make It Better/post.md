## Caption
"Make it better" is the worst thing you can say to an AI agent.

Better how? It'll guess, and rewrite everything to match the guess.

Good feedback has 3 parts:
1. What's wrong: "The page takes 3s to load"
2. Why it matters: "Users drop before pricing shows"
3. What good looks like: "Under 1s, same layout"

Same way I give feedback to designers at work. Works on agents for the same reason.

Send this to someone whose agent keeps "improving" the wrong thing.
