## Caption
My AI agent kept making decisions that were fine for today and wrong for next month.

It only sees the ticket in front of it, so it hardcodes one currency, one role, one language. That's fine, unless next month's feature is multi-currency.

The fix is a PM classic: a roadmap. A short ROADMAP.md in the repo with Now / Next / Later, and one rule: "Don't build Next. Just don't block it."

A ticket tells the agent what to do. A roadmap tells it what not to break.
