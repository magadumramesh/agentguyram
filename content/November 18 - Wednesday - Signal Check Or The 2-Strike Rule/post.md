## Caption
If your AI agent fails the same fix twice, stop arguing with it.

By strike three the session is full of wrong attempts, and the agent keeps building on its own bad ideas.

The 2-strike rule:
1. Ask it to write down what it tried and why it failed
2. /clear
3. Start fresh with that note plus the one thing you now know

A fresh start with a lesson beats a long session with a grudge.

Send this to someone who's been in the same chat for 3 hours.
