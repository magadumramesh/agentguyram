## Caption
The most important skill in the age of AI agents isn't prompting. It isn't coding. It's writing.

Not pretty writing. Clear writing.

Every agent failure I've had comes down to a sentence I didn't think through. "Make it better." Better how? "Add auth." Which kind, for whom?

At work, I've watched the clearest writers become the best leaders. Now they're becoming the best builders.

My one habit: before I hit enter, I reread my prompt as if a stranger wrote it.
