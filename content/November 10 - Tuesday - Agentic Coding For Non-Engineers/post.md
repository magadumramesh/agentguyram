**S3 · PMs**
- Build: clickable prototypes to test an idea before a sprint
- Without it: "We spent 2 sprints building something users didn't want."
- DO THIS TODAY: prototype your next feature idea before writing the PRD.

**S4 · Designers**
- Build: real front-end versions of your mocks, with real states (empty, loading, error)
- Without it: "The handoff lost every hover state."

**S5 · Analysts**
- Build: data-cleaning scripts, one-off dashboards, SQL you can actually read
- Without it: "3 hours in a spreadsheet for a 20-line script."

**S6 · Ops / everyone else**
- Build: internal tools. The form, the report, the thing that "IT will get to next quarter".

**S7 · The 3 rules for non-engineers**
1. Start in a sandbox, never the production repo
2. Ask it to explain everything it does
3. Have an engineer review before anything goes live

**S8 · Cheat sheet**
