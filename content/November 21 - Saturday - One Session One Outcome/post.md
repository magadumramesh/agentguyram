## Caption
Scope creep kills projects at work. It kills AI agent sessions too.

"Fix the signup bug." → "while you're there, rename that" → "tidy the CSS?" → "add a loading state"… 20 minutes later nothing works and you can't tell which change broke it.

My rule: one session, one outcome.
New idea mid-task → TODO file, not the chat.
Ship the one thing → commit → /clear → next.

Send this to the friend whose agent session has 12 "while you're at it"s.
