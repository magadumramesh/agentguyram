## Caption
Everyone's calling everything an "AI agent". The actual difference:

💬 Chatbot: you ask, it answers
🔀 Workflow: follows steps a developer wrote in advance
🔁 Agent: the model decides its own next step, loops until the goal is met

The test: who decides what happens next? You, the developer in advance, or the model at runtime?

Workflows aren't worse. When the path is predictable, they're cheaper and safer.
