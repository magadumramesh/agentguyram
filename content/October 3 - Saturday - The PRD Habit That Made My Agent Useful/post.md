## Caption
I write product specs for a living. One section of them fixed my AI coding agent: acceptance criteria.

I used to give agents one-liners like "add password reset". They guessed, and they guessed confidently.

Now every task gets 2–4 checkable lines that say what "done" means. The agent knows when to stop, and I know what to test.

Your agent isn't guessing because it's dumb. It's guessing because nobody told it what done looks like.

Send this to the PM who thinks they can't build with AI. They're better at it than they think.
